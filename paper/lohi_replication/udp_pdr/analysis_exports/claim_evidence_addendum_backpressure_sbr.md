## Recommended Thesis Wording Snippets

```text
The Backpressure comparator is implemented as a simplified ISL routing reference. It uses a destination-proxy commodity model and computes next-hop pressure from queue-differential information. In the H80 experiment, the effective queue source is node-total queue bytes, with shortest-path fallback and a forward-progress loop guard.
```

```text
In the H80 60-second hotspot scenario, LHTR improves aggregate PDR from 66.25% to 88.68% and focus-flow PDR from 44.39% to 92.93% relative to the simplified Backpressure reference.
```

```text
Although SBR decisions are rare globally, the diagnostic sample shows that they concentrate on the target corridor: the H80 60-second target-corridor SBR ratio is 14.68%, compared with a formal global ratio of 0.116%.
```

Safe caveat wording:

